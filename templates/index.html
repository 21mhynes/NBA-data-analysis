What the results suggest

The raw advantage mostly fades after late-game free throws are removed

Teams with more bonus attempts won much more often in the raw comparison. Excluding the final two minutes of the fourth quarter and overtime narrows that gap. This is consistent with intentional fouling adding free throws for teams that are already ahead.

These are associations, not proof that bonus free throws cause wins. Home-court advantage, game score, and late-game strategy also affect the results. Overtime findings have a smaller sample.
