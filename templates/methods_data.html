Bonus-entry definition

Team-foul penalty is tracked by period

A team enters the bonus when its opponent reaches the applicable team-foul limit in that period. The analysis separately accounts for the special final-two-minute foul rule.

Technical, flagrant, and clear-path free throws are excluded from standard bonus free-throw totals.

Two first-to-bonus measures

Keep the definitions distinct

More periods first: compare each team's first bonus entry in each period. A team has the game-level advantage if it reaches the bonus first in more periods than its opponent; ties remain a separate outcome.

First anywhere in the game: identify which team records the earliest bonus entry in the game. This is a different measure from the period-count comparison.

By-quarter: compare which team first enters the bonus within each individual quarter or overtime period.

Late-game adjustment

Adjusted attempts are a sensitivity comparison

The adjusted analysis removes bonus free throws during the final two minutes of the fourth quarter and overtime. This helps show how late-game fouling changes the raw attempt relationship; it does not remove all game-situation or team-quality effects.

Interpretation

Associations do not establish cause

Team quality, score at the time of the foul, home-court advantage, opponent style, quarter, intentional fouling, and differences across NBA eras may influence both bonus opportunities and game outcomes.
