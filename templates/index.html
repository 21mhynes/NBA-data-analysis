Conclusion and limits

Bonus statistics are connected to winning, but game context matters

Reaching the bonus first is associated with a small win advantage. Attempt and accuracy patterns differ, and the attempt relationship is sensitive to late-game strategy. These results do not prove that bonus opportunities cause teams to win.

Limitations include home-court advantage, intentional fouling, smaller overtime samples, team and rule changes over time, and differences in the eras being compared.
